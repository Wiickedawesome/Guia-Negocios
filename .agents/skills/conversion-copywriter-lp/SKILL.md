# Conversion Copywriting & Landing Page Skill

This skill provides direct-response copywriting frameworks and structural templates to power high-converting landing pages for **GUIA DE NEGOCIOS** (`/admin/landing-pages.astro`).

---

## 1. Core Psychological Drivers of Hispanic Entrepreneurs

To achieve high conversion rates, copy must address the specific anxieties and aspirations of Hispanic business owners:
1. **Fear of Legal & IRS Mistakes:** Dread of receiving letters from the IRS due to language barriers or misunderstanding tax laws.
2. **Language Barrier & Jargon Overwhelm:** Intimidation caused by complex American legal terminology (*Statutory Agent, Articles of Organization, Operating Agreement, Franchise Tax*).
3. **Desire for Legitimacy & Growth:** Pride in formalizing a family business, accessing American bank credit, and winning commercial contracts.
4. **Relatability & Trust:** High value placed on direct personal connection, bilingual guidance, and phone/WhatsApp accessibility over impersonal corporate bots.

---

## 2. The 7-Block High-Conversion Landing Page Blueprint

### Block 1: Above-the-Fold Hero Section
* **Pre-Headline:** Badge with social proof (e.g., `⭐ Asesoría Empresarial 100% en Español`).
* **H1 Value Proposition:** Clear outcome in plain Spanish:
  * *Formula:* `[Verbo de Acción] + [Resultado Deseado] + [Sin la Frustración Común] + [En Tiempo Récord]`.
  * *Example:* "Registre su Empresa en EE.UU. y Obtenga su EIN sin Complicaciones Legales ni Miedo al IRS."
* **Subheadline:** 2 sentences expanding the benefit and providing reassurance.
* **Dual Primary CTAs:**
  * Primary: `[Agendar Consulta Estratégica Gratuita →]`
  * Secondary (Direct): `[Llamar a un Asesor: (865) 202-7112]`
* **Trust Badges Bar:** 5M+ Empresas | Asesoría Bilingüe | Blindaje Legal 100%.

### Block 2: Problem & Agitation Section (PAS Framework)
* **Problem:** Identify the current struggle (operating without LLC, mixed finances, fear of losing personal savings).
* **Agitation:** Detail the hidden costs (IRS $25,000 penalties, personal lawsuits taking the family home, bank accounts frozen).
* **Solution Transition:** "No tiene que navegar las leyes comerciales de EE.UU. en solitario."

### Block 3: The 3-Step Simple Solution
1. **Paso 1: Diagnóstico Estratégico:** Analizamos su tipo de negocio y seleccionamos el mejor estado y estructura fiscal.
2. **Paso 2: Registro & Blindaje Total:** Tramitamos su LLC, número EIN ante el IRS y licencias oficiales sin que usted llene formularios confusos.
3. **Paso 3: Lanzamiento & Banca Comercial:** Le ayudamos a abrir su cuenta bancaria comercial y dejar su empresa lista para facturar.

### Block 4: Real-World Social Proof & Case Studies
* Showcase concrete before/after transformations with names, industries (construcción, limpieza, e-commerce, consultoría), and specific metrics (e.g. *"$42,000 en contratos comerciales conseguidos tras obtener el EIN"*).

### Block 5: Transparent Package & Pricing Matrix
* Clear breakdown of what is included, timeline, and zero-hidden-fee guarantees.

### Block 6: Risk-Reversal FAQ Accordion
* Tackle the top 5 objections:
  * *"¿Puedo abrir mi empresa si no tengo Número de Seguro Social (SSN)?"*
  * *"¿Cuánto tiempo tarda el proceso?"*
  * *"¿Qué pasa si vivo fuera de Estados Unidos?"*
  * *"¿Ustedes me atienden totalmente en español?"*

### Block 7: Urgency & Final Action CTA
* Clear closing incentive to contact now with phone number, WhatsApp link, and quick contact form.
