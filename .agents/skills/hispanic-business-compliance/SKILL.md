# Hispanic Business Legal & Regulatory Compliance Skill

This skill provides authoritative guidelines on entity formation, IRS tax compliance, state registration, banking, and strict statutory requirements for both **domestic Hispanic business owners** and **international Non-Resident Aliens (NRA)**.

---

## 1. Non-Resident Aliens (NRA) & Foreign Ownership in the U.S.

### A. What Foreign Non-Residents CAN Do (Rights & Advantages)
1. **100% Remote Ownership:** Any non-U.S. citizen living outside the U.S. can 100% own a U.S. business (LLC or C-Corporation) without ever visiting the U.S. or holding a visa.
2. **State Selection Freedom:** NRAs can form their entity in any U.S. state (most popular: Wyoming for low fees & privacy, Delaware for venture capital, Florida/Texas for trade, Tennessee for logistics).
3. **EIN Without SSN:** NRAs can obtain a federal Employer Identification Number (EIN) from the IRS without an SSN or ITIN by submitting **Form SS-4** via the IRS International Fax Line with `"FOREIGN"` on Line 7b.
4. **Remote U.S. Banking & Payments:** Access to U.S. fintech banking (Mercury, Relay, Wise Business) and global payment processors (Stripe, PayPal, Authorize.net) using a valid passport and Articles of Organization.

### B. Strict Legal & Tax Limitations for Non-Resident Aliens (CRITICAL)
1. **[RESTRICCIÓN ESTATUTARIA] Prohibición Estricta de Corporaciones S (S-Corp):**
   - **Internal Revenue Code § 1361(b)(1)(C)** strictly prohibits Non-Resident Aliens from being shareholders in an S-Corporation. An LLC owned by an NRA **CANNOT elect S-Corp tax status**. Attempting to do so immediately invalidates the election.
2. **[REQUISITO MIGRATORIO] Prohibición de Trabajo Físico en Suelo Estadounidense sin Visa:**
   - An NRA can own, direct, and receive profits from a U.S. business from abroad. However, they **CANNOT perform physical labor or receive W-2 wages inside the United States** without an appropriate work visa (e.g. E-2 Investor Visa, L-1, O-1, or H-1B).
3. **[RIESGO FISCAL CRÍTICO] La Trampa del Formulario 5472 del IRS (Reporte Anual Obligatorio de $25,000):**
   - A U.S. Single-Member LLC owned 100% by a foreign non-resident is classified as a *"Disregarded Entity for income tax, but a Corporation for reporting purposes"* under IRS Section 6038A.
   - **Mandatory Filing:** Must file **Form 5472 attached to a pro-forma Form 1120** by **April 15th** of every year, detailing all reportable transactions (capital contributions, distributions, formation fees, loans), even if the company had **$0 in revenue**.
   - **Statutory Penalty:** The IRS imposes a mandatory minimum penalty of **$25,000 USD per year** for failure to file, late filing, or incomplete Form 5472.
4. **[NORMATIVA TRIBUTARIA] Tributación Federal (ECI vs. FDAP):**
   - **Effectively Connected Income (ECI):** If the foreign owner has a "U.S. Trade or Business" (ETBUS) with physical dependent agents or offices in the U.S., profits are subject to regular U.S. federal graduated tax rates, requiring an ITIN and personal Form 1040-NR.
   - **Passive / FDAP Income:** Royalties, dividends, or passive income from U.S. sources are subject to a **30% gross withholding tax**, unless reduced by a bilateral tax treaty (e.g., Spain, Mexico, Chile).

## 2. Domestic Hispanic Business Owners (U.S. Residents)

### Entity Structuring Matrix:

| Structure | Best For | Tax Treatment | Asset Protection | Self-Employment Tax |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sole Proprietorship (Trabajador Independiente)** | Side hustles, test projects | Pass-through to Schedule C | **NONE** (Personal assets at 100% risk) | Full 15.3% SE Tax on all net income |
| **Single-Member LLC** | Solopreneurs, contractors, services | Pass-through (Disregarded Entity) | **HIGH** (Corporate veil shields home, personal savings) | Full 15.3% SE Tax on net profits |
| **Multi-Member LLC** | Partnerships, family businesses | Partnership (Form 1065 / K-1) | **HIGH** (Charging order protection) | 15.3% SE Tax for active managing members |
| **LLC with S-Corp Election** | Businesses with net profit > $60k/yr | Pass-through (Form 1120-S / K-1) | **HIGH** | **Significant savings**: SE tax paid ONLY on reasonable W-2 salary, rest as distributions |

## 3. IRS Form SS-4: EIN Application Protocols

### Procedure for Domestic Owners (with SSN/ITIN):
- Instant online issuance via the IRS Online EIN Portal (`irs.gov/ein`) between 7:00 AM and 10:00 PM EST.

### Procedure for Foreign Owners (without SSN/ITIN):
1. Complete Form SS-4 (Rev. December 2023).
2. **Line 7b (SSN/ITIN):** Write `"FOREIGN"`.
3. **Line 9a (Type of Entity):** Check `"Other"` and write `"Foreign-Owned Disregarded Entity"` or `"Limited Liability Company"`.
4. Transmit via Fax to the IRS International Operations Unit:
   - Fax from outside the U.S.: `+1-304-707-9471`
   - Fax from within the U.S.: `+1-855-641-6935`
5. Processing time: 4 to 7 business days via fax.

## 4. Corporate Transparency Act & FinCEN BOI Compliance
* **Mandatory Requirement:** All U.S. entities (domestic and foreign-owned) must file a Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI) Report with the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN).
* **Foreign Owners:** Must upload an unexpired, high-resolution copy of their valid foreign passport and report residential address.
* **Deadlines:** Within 90 days of formation for entities created in 2024; within 30 days for entities created in 2025 onwards.
* **Civil Penalties:** Up to $591 per day for willful non-compliance.

## 5. Multi-State Quick Reference Guide

* **Tennessee:** SOS filing $300 minimum ($50/member up to $3,000). Standard Business Tax ($15) at County Clerk + TN Department of Revenue Sales & Franchise/Excise tax.
* **Wyoming:** $100 filing fee, $60 annual report. No state income tax, excellent privacy for foreign and domestic owners.
* **Delaware:** $90 filing fee, $300 annual franchise tax. Premier corporate law court (Court of Chancery).
* **Florida:** $125 filing fee, $138.75 annual report on Sunbiz by May 1st. High Hispanic market density.
* **Texas:** $300 filing fee. No state personal income tax, Texas Franchise Tax No Tax Due report required.
