# Social Media Content Repurposing Skill

This skill allows agents to take any long-form blog article or business guide from **GUIA DE NEGOCIOS** and convert it into multi-platform social media assets.

---

## 1. 7-Slide Instagram & Facebook Carousel Template

* **Slide 1 (Cover / Hook):**
  * *Headline:* Big, high-curiosity hook in bold text.
  * *Subtext:* "Lo que el 90% de los emprendedores no sabe sobre [Tema]."
  * *Visual Cue:* Bold brand colors + "Desliza para ver la guía 👉".
* **Slide 2 (The Hidden Trap / Problem):**
  * Concrete mistake (e.g., "Operar como persona física pone en riesgo tus ahorros y tu casa").
* **Slide 3 (Step 1 of the Solution):**
  * Clean bullet point with green checkmark + actionable advice.
* **Slide 4 (Step 2 of the Solution):**
  * Actionable advice on taxes or entity structure.
* **Slide 5 (Stat / Industry Metric Card):**
  * Bold metric (e.g., "$653B generado por empresarios hispanos").
* **Slide 6 (Pro-Tip / Warning):**
  * Critical IRS or state compliance reminder.
* **Slide 7 (CTA Slide):**
  * "Guarda este post para cuando registres tu negocio 📌 | Comenta 'GUIA' y te enviamos la lista completa de requisitos por mensaje directo."
